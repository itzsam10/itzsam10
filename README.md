## Selected engineering projects

### [NeuroKey - Assistive Biosignal Controller](https://github.com/itzsam10/neurokey-assistive-controller) · Ongoing
A research prototype exploring hands-free assistive control for people with severe motor impairments.

- Demonstrated laptop-side forearm EMG acquisition and exploratory signal analysis using ChordsPy BLE, LSL, Python, SciPy, and scikit-learn.
- Current work is focused on repeatable EMG trials and event logic.
- EOG navigation, EEG/SSVEP selection, ESP32 outputs, and integrated device control remain future development stages.

This is an early research prototype, not a clinically validated or safety-critical control system.

### [ESP32 Air Pollution Exposure Chamber Controller](https://github.com/itzsam10/Urban-air-pollution-controller)
Contributed electronics integration and ESP32 firmware for a collaborative M.Pharm exposure-chamber prototype.

- Integrated particulate and environmental sensors, RTC, LCD, microSD logging, and PWM blower control.
- Documented the wiring, system architecture, firmware behavior, and project limitations.
- My contribution was the electronics and control subsystem; biological testing was conducted by the collaborating researcher.
